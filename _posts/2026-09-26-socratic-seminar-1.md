Welcome to BitDevs Zaria's first Socratic Seminar. This session brings together
developers, students, and Bitcoin enthusiasts to explore the fundamentals of
Bitcoin development through collaborative learning and open technical
discussion.

## Details

- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Format:** Technical learning and Socratic Seminar
- **Date:** September 26, 2026
- **Time:** 11:00 AM–1:00 PM
- **Location:** BT Hub, GRA Zaria
- **Registration:** [Register on Luma](https://luma.com/zc84r9wn)

## Agenda

### Introduction to the Community

- Understand the mission of BitDevs and BitDevs Zaria.
- Explore the purpose of Socratic Seminars and how they encourage technical
  discussion.
- Discover opportunities for developers to learn, collaborate, and contribute
  to the Bitcoin ecosystem.

### Socratic Seminar: Understanding Bitcoin

- Explore the fundamentals of Bitcoin and how the network works.
- Understand decentralization, nodes, and consensus.
- Learn about Bitcoin transactions and the role of developers in the ecosystem.

#### Learning resources

- *Mastering Bitcoin* — Introduction
- *Mastering Bitcoin* — The Bitcoin Network
- Bitcoin Developer Guide

### Open Technical Discussion

- Share questions, perspectives, and ideas about Bitcoin development.
- Discuss technical challenges and opportunities within the Bitcoin ecosystem.
- Explore open-source contributions and developer learning opportunities.
- Suggest topics for future Socratic Seminars.
