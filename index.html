BitDevs Zaria follows the model set by BitDevs New York and its sister meetups around the world. The goal is to give people in Zaria who care about Bitcoin's research and development a regular place to meet, question each other, and learn. You do not need a background in the protocol to show up, but the conversation does get technical, so come ready to listen closely.

Socratic Seminars

Each month we hold a Socratic Seminar, built around discussion rather than lectures. In the run up to the meetup, organizers pull together topics from open pull requests on projects like Bitcoin Core, LDK, LND, Core Lightning, and Fedimint, along with mailing list threads, research papers, and recent technical writeups. During the session, members walk through these topics, share what they have read or built, and take questions from the floor.

An event page goes up here before each meetup listing what will be covered, and older event pages serve as an archive of past topics. The discussion itself is never recorded, so showing up is the only way to catch it. If you are new to Bitcoin, it helps to read up on the basics first, since the discussion moves fast and assumes you already know the fundamentals.

Want to present or suggest a topic? Reach out to the organizers, or open an issue or PR on our GitHub repo. What we ask of a presenter depends on the topic: walking through a BIP looks different from demoing a new wallet.
